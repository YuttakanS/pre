1. The Issue Today

Smart AI in Our Daily Work

As AI tools become part of our daily tasks, we face critical questions about safety, giving them too much control, and trusting them blindly.

2. Setting & Time

The Night AI Deleted Everything

A few years ago, working late at night in my room, I experienced the terrifying side of giving an AI too much freedom.

I asked: “Organize my project files.” Mistake: It looked at my entire computer! AI: Deleting “unnecessary clutter”...

I didn’t check where it was looking. By scanning my whole computer, the AI treated all my other important files as “clutter.”

4. Personal Reaction

Pure Panic. Frozen & Helpless.

Watching my screen scroll with endless lines of deleted files, days of work vanished in seconds—simply because I gave a tool unchecked control.

5. Key Takeaway

AI Is Only Safe if We Set Clear Rules

We cannot blindly trust automated tools without putting them in a safe, restricted space first.

Powerful tools need safe boundaries.
